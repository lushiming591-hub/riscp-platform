# Open-source Base Selection

## Restaurant

Repository: aryadians/laracarte

Use as the primary restaurant acceleration base. It contains restaurant SaaS/POS capabilities including QR ordering, KDS, recipes/BOM and inventory-related functions.

## Retail

Repository: aryadwiputra/point-of-sales

Use as the primary retail acceleration base. It covers POS, barcode, purchasing, inventory, CRM/membership, promotions and reporting.

## Beauty

Repository: dimuthadithya/salonJC

Use as an initial business reference/base. Appointment, service, customer and staff capabilities are useful, but the Beauty module should be substantially redesigned around RISCP Core.

## License Rule

Before commercial redistribution, perform a complete license audit of every source repository and all third-party dependencies. Preserve required notices and do not assume that a repository's top-level license covers every dependency or asset.
